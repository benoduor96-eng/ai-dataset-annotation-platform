# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and assume good faith while discussing technical disagreements.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give actionable feedback and respect different experience levels.
- Protect confidential information and respect the licenses and attribution of others.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and handled with discretion where possible. Maintainers may edit or remove comments,
close discussions, or restrict participation for conduct that violates this policy.
